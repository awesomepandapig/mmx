# MMX

MMX is self-hosted exchange infrastructure for MMO economies: price-time matching, a replicated execution record, market data, and signed settlement notifications. Deploy it beside your game servers; your game keeps custody of assets and owns its ledger.

Use limit and market orders for interchangeable commodities such as materials, consumables, and currency pairs. MMX reports executions; your application reserves balances, applies transfers, and decides its own fees and economic rules.

## Start here

1. Install with [Helm](/installation/helm) on Kubernetes, use [Local Docker](/installation/local), or [build from source](/installation/source).
2. Follow [Getting started](/getting-started) to inspect the cluster, sign in, create a market, and trade.
3. Connect [REST orders](/api/rest) and [signed settlement webhooks](/guides/webhooks) to your game's ledger.
